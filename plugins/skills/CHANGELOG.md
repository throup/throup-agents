# Changelog

All notable changes to the `skills` plugin. Versions follow [semver](https://semver.org/).

## 0.2.0

- `skill-audit`: the blind reviewer states what the skill is for from the cut alone, before the
  confirmed purpose, the baseline and the skill's artefacts are sent to it; the report shows that
  reading beside the purpose.
- `skill-audit`: every edit made after the blind review goes to a new blind reviewer before the
  audit is done, on the tracked and the untracked route; its findings go to the operator.

## 0.1.0

- `skill-audit`: cut one skill to the sentences its confirmed purpose needs and no authority
  already states, then have a fresh blind reviewer judge the cut against that purpose and a
  baseline.
